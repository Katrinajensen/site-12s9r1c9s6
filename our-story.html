Our story

We just wanted a vitamin we didn't dread taking.

Most gummy vitamins are candy dressed up as health, sweetened with fructose and sized like a treat. We wanted the fun of a fruit snack without that tradeoff — so we set out to build one.

Photo: Ufoma Ojo on Unsplash

Why fructose-free

Same fun format, different sweetener

The problem

Gummy vitamins solved the "I don't want to swallow a pill" problem, but most did it by leaning on fructose-heavy syrups — trading one downside for another.

Our approach

We formulate without fructose from the start, rather than bolting a "sugar-free" label onto an existing recipe.

Still a treat

It's still a gummy you look forward to, packaged single-serve like a fruit snack pouch — just without the ingredient we set out to avoid.

Built the right way

Why one pouch has 8 different gummies

Instead of one catch-all multivitamin gummy, each daily pouch has 8 distinct gummies — Vitamin C, D3, a multivitamin blend, and elderberry in every line, plus four more tuned to Women's, Men's, or Kids' needs. It's more work to formulate than a single recipe, but it means every nutrient gets its own properly dosed gummy instead of being crammed into one.
